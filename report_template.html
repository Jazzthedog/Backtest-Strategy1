Method & provenance

WHAT THIS SHOWS — Exactly how to reproduce every number on this page.

Rules

Enter long when the daily close exceeds the highest high of the prior 20 bars. Exit to flat when the daily close falls below the lowest low of the prior 10 bars. Strict inequalities; the current bar is excluded from both channels. Long or flat only, never short.

Execution

Fills at the signal bar's close. Position = 10% of current equity, recomputed at each entry. One position at a time, entered and exited whole. No leverage. Idle cash earns 0%.

Prices

Yahoo raw OHLC with auto_adjust=False — split-adjusted but not dividend-adjusted, so all four fields share one scale. Adj Close deliberately unused for signals. Dividends credited as cash while long.

Reproduce

.venv/Scripts/python.exe backtest.py → writes qqq_raw.csv (data snapshot) and results.json. Then build_report.py regenerates this page. No backtesting library; the loop is explicit in backtest.py.
